# Account Deletion Request

**App:** LifeBalance  
**Developer:** mylife.myplan  
**Contact email:** lifebalance.app.help@gmail.com

Users can delete their account directly in the app:

1. Open Settings  
2. Tap Delete account  
3. Confirm deletion (password may be required for re-authentication)

## What data is deleted

When account deletion is completed, we delete:

1. Authentication account (Firebase Auth)  
2. Cloud user data (Firestore/Storage)  
3. Local app data on the device (app database and related profile data)

## Data retention

We do not keep active personal account data after successful deletion.  
Backup/log retention, if any, follows our internal operational/security policy for a limited period.

## Partial deletion

Partial deletion without deleting the account is not provided.
